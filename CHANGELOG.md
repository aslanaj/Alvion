# Changelog

## v5 — 2026-11-15

- **Compact grids.** Bento grid groups size themselves to the screen height and fit one laptop screen.
- **Compact visualizers.** Estimator, PPF, vinyl wrap and tint visualizers fit one desktop screen, with the car on the left and choices and quote on the right. They are also a little more compact on phones.
- **Vehicle row.** The estimator's vehicles sit in one scrolling row with arrow buttons.
- **PPF colors by brand.** Color PPF swatches are grouped into PurePPF and XPEL.
- **Press feedback.** Every tappable control reacts when pressed, including inside the visualizers.
- **Mobile nav bar motion.** A sliding highlight and icon pop, modeled on Instagram's nav bar. Pages cross-fade on phones.
- **Headlines.** Fixed the headline spacing on the three local pages.
- **Tests.** Added `tests/static_check.py` and `tests/browser_check.js`.

## v4 — 2026-11-13 (live)

- **Quote sheet anywhere.** Any page address ending in `#quote` opens the quote sheet; used for the Instagram bio link.
- **Accent.** Ice-blue accent on primary buttons with a press-down edge and ripple; dark greys tinted toward the logo navy.
- **Share image.** A new image, from the Infiniti Q60 project photo.
- **Estimator rebuilt.** Paint maps for nine vehicles, full-panel recoloring, 3M / Avery Dennison vinyl and PurePPF / XPEL color PPF.
- **PPF visualizer.** Color PPF repaints the car through a paint map. The separate clear-finish row was removed.
- **Opening from a folder.** Visualizers work when the site is opened from a folder.
- **Contact form.** The contact page's reply choices no longer overlap.
- **Material brands.** Window tint: XPEL. PPF: XPEL, PurePPF, STEK. Vinyl: 3M, Avery Dennison.
- **Local pages.** New pages for Tesla wraps in Los Angeles, color change wraps in South Bay and car wraps in Long Beach, with structured data, sitemap entries and footer links.
- The mobile nav dot was removed.
