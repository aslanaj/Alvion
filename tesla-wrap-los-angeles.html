Color-change wraps for your Tesla.

ALVION installs full color-change vinyl wraps on Teslas for owners across Los Angeles, Orange County, South Bay and Long Beach. We use 3M and Avery Dennison films in gloss, satin and matte, and every job comes with a written quote and a 5-year installation warranty.

A Tesla has large, smooth panels and very little trim, so the finish you pick does most of the talking. Send your model and the color you have in mind, and we reply with the material, coverage, price and timing in writing.

Wrap, PPF or both?

Vinyl wrap changes the color or finish of your Tesla. It usually takes 3–5 days and carries a 5-year installation warranty.

Paint protection film (PPF) is built to guard the paint against road debris and light scratches. Full-front PPF takes about 1–2 days, full-body 3–5 days, with a 10-year installation warranty. We install XPEL, PurePPF and STEK.

Color PPF does both jobs in one film: a new color with the protection of PPF. Many owners also add XPEL window tint or a ceramic coating in the same visit.

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

How much does a Tesla wrap cost in Los Angeles?

A full color-change wrap on a Tesla starts at $2,990, and our founding offer starts at $1,990. The final price depends on the model, film and paint condition and is confirmed in writing before booking.

How long does it take to wrap a Tesla?

A full wrap usually takes 3–5 days. Surface preparation, removal of old film and custom details can change the schedule; timing is confirmed in your quote.

Will a wrap damage my Tesla's paint?

Factory paint in good condition is fine under vinyl. Repainted panels, chips and peeling clear coat can lift during removal, so we assess the surface before quoting. Removal is quoted separately.

How long does a vinyl wrap last?

Quality wrap films from 3M and Avery Dennison typically last around 5 to 7 years. Strong sun, outdoor parking and harsh car washes shorten that. Installation is covered by a 5-year warranty.

Can you wrap a Cybertruck?

Yes. Cybertruck full wraps start at $2,990. Whether a vehicle qualifies for the founding offer is confirmed in your written quote.

Should I wrap my Tesla or add PPF?

Vinyl changes the color; PPF protects the paint from road debris and light scratches. Color PPF does both, starting at $5,690 for a Tesla. You can also wrap now and add PPF to the front later.

How soon can I wash my Tesla after a wrap?

Wait 5–7 days before the first wash, then hand wash gently. Your aftercare guide covers the rest.

Which areas do you serve?

Los Angeles, Orange County, South Bay and Long Beach, by appointment, Monday to Sunday, 10 AM–8 PM.
