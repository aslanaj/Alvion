GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Does PPF prevent every scratch or chip?

No. PPF helps protect against road debris and light scratches but cannot prevent deep impacts or accident damage. Self-healing properties depend on the film and do not repair damage beneath it.

Should I choose full-front or full-body coverage?

Full-front PPF focuses on common impact areas. Full-body PPF extends coverage to the agreed exterior panels. Your driving, desired finish and budget help determine the scope.

How long does PPF installation take?

Full-front PPF usually takes about 1–2 days; full-body PPF usually takes 3–5 days. Preparation and coverage can change the schedule, which is confirmed in your quote.
