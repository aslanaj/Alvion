GOOD
QUESTIONS.

How much does a car wrap cost in Long Beach?

Full color changes start at $2,990 for a Tesla, $3,890 for a sedan or coupe and $4,490 for an SUV. The final price is confirmed in writing before booking.

Can I wrap and tint at the same time?

Yes. Many customers combine a wrap with window tint or a ceramic coating in one visit.

How long does a wrap take?

A full wrap usually takes 3–5 days. Window tint is usually done the same day.

Is vinyl the same as PPF?

No. Vinyl primarily changes color or finish. Paint protection film is designed to help protect against road debris and light scratches.

Will a wrap damage my paint?

Factory paint in good condition is fine under vinyl. Repainted panels, chips and peeling clear coat can lift during removal, so we assess the surface before quoting. Removal is quoted separately.

How soon can I wash it?

Wait 5–7 days before washing a new wrap or PPF, then hand wash gently.
