GOOD
QUESTIONS.

How much does detailing cost?

Detailing is individually quoted by vehicle, condition and scope. Send the year, make, model and photos of the areas you want addressed for a written price.

Can detailing remove every stain or scratch?

No. Results depend on the material and depth or age of the damage. We assess the areas of concern and discuss realistic expectations before booking.

Can you detail a wrapped or protected vehicle?

Tell us what film or coating is installed and its condition. We confirm suitable cleaning and care methods before agreeing the work.

How long will you need the car?

Timing depends on the condition and agreed interior, exterior or correction work. Your quote confirms the schedule and drop-off details.
