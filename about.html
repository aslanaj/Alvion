ABOUT ALVION

AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN
& CUSTOMIZATION.

ALVION brings together vehicle design, protection, and personal expression.

A passion for cars.
A vision of our own.

ALVION began with Aslan AJ’s passion for cars and design. What started with personalizing his own vehicles grew into a desire to help others bring their ideas to life.

We believe a car should reflect the person behind the wheel. Through color, materials, and carefully considered details, we turn your vision into something that feels distinctly yours.

Customization is our foundation. As ALVION grows, so does our ambition—to explore new possibilities in automotive design and create something unmistakably our own.

How a project
works.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your car

    Send the year, make and model, a few photos, and what you want to change.

  2. 02

    Get a written price

    Starting prices are published on our site. Your quote confirms the material, finish, coverage and applicable warranty before booking.

  3. 03

    Book your appointment

    Work is by appointment only and is carried out at a shared automotive shop. We serve South Bay & Long Beach; the service address and drop-off details are confirmed before booking.

  4. 04

    Installation and aftercare

    We install your project. Our aftercare guide covers curing times and washing, and our installation warranty covers faults caused by our installation.
