GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Does the starting price include the windshield or roof?

No. The listed package covers sides and rear glass. Windshield, sunroof and panoramic glass are priced separately for the vehicle and selected film.

What does VLT mean?

Visible light transmission is the percentage of light passing through. Lower VLT appears darker. The preview is an illustration; actual appearance depends on glass, film and lighting.

When can I roll the windows down?

Keep the windows up for 3–5 days while the film cures, following the care instructions supplied with your installation.

How long does tint installation take?

Window tint is usually completed the same day. The selected glass coverage and any existing film removal can affect timing; your quote confirms the schedule.
