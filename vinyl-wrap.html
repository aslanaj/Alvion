GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Will a vinyl wrap damage my paint?

Paint condition matters. Repainted panels, chips and peeling clear coat can lift during removal. We assess the surface before quoting and discuss limitations; removal is quoted separately.

Is vinyl wrap the same as PPF?

No. Vinyl primarily changes color or finish. Paint protection film is designed to help protect against road debris and light scratches. Choose based on the appearance and protection you want.

How long does a full wrap take?

A full wrap usually takes 3–5 days. Surface preparation, removal and custom details can change the schedule; timing is confirmed in your quote.
