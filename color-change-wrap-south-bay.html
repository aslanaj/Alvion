GOOD
QUESTIONS.

How much does a color change wrap cost in the South Bay?

Full color changes start at $2,990 for a Tesla, $3,890 for a sedan or coupe and $4,490 for an SUV. The final price is confirmed in writing before booking.

How long does a full wrap take?

A full wrap usually takes 3–5 days. Surface preparation, removal and custom details can change the schedule; timing is confirmed in your quote.

Will a wrap damage my paint?

Factory paint in good condition is fine under vinyl. Repainted panels, chips and peeling clear coat can lift during removal, so we assess the surface before quoting. Removal is quoted separately.

How long does a wrap last?

Quality wrap films from 3M and Avery Dennison typically last around 5 to 7 years. Strong sun, outdoor parking and harsh car washes shorten that.

Can I wrap just part of the car?

Yes. Roofs, mirrors, hoods, stripes and chrome delete are priced individually. Send a photo and what you want changed.

Which South Bay cities do you serve?

Torrance, Redondo Beach, Manhattan Beach, Hermosa Beach, El Segundo, Gardena, Carson and Palos Verdes, along with the rest of Los Angeles, Orange County and Long Beach.
