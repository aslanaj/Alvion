Who we are

ALVION LLC (“ALVION,” “we”) operates alvionperformance.com and provides vehicle wrap, paint protection film, window tint, ceramic coating and detailing services in South Bay and Long Beach, California. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you request a quote, call, text, email or message us on social media, we receive what you choose to share, such as:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • How you’d like us to reply (text, call or email)
  • Your vehicle details, the service you’re interested in and your project notes
  • Photos of your vehicle you upload or send

We use this only to answer your request, prepare quotes, schedule and carry out work, provide aftercare and warranty support, and keep business records.

Cookies and analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (for example, which pages are viewed and whether the call, text or quote buttons are used). Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type. We do not use advertising cookies.

Analytics only runs after you click “Accept” on the cookie banner. You can change your choice anytime with . Your choice is stored in your browser. Learn more in how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of visit) for security and reliability.

How we share information

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising. We only share it with service providers who help us run the business — such as our website host, email provider and Google Analytics — or when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and customer records for as long as needed to provide services, honor warranties and meet legal and tax obligations, then delete them.

Your California privacy rights

California residents may request to know what personal information we hold about them, ask us to correct or delete it, and opt out of any sale or sharing (we do neither). We will not treat you differently for making a request. To make a request, email alvionperformance@gmail.com.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or email system is completely secure.

Children

Our site and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions? Email alvionperformance@gmail.com or call (909) 450-6843.
